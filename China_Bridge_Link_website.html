About China Bridge Link

A reliable connection between students and China.

China Bridge Link provides student services in China and works with international students seeking educational opportunities at Chinese universities. We coordinate key steps and help students understand what to expect before and after arrival.

  • Guidance through university admission steps
  • Admission and visa document coordination
  • Arrival, registration and residence-permit support

Our commitment

Clear communication. Coordinated support.

We aim to make each stage understandable, from the first enquiry to the student’s arrival in China. Important service and commission terms should be confirmed in writing.
